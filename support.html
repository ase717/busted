Busted: Support

Need help with Busted? Write to asestudio21@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

When you write, please tell us your phone model, your iOS or Android version, the app version (Settings in your phone, then Busted) and what happened.

Common questions

How do I play? Choose a pack, add 3 to 12 players and decide how many of them get a mission. Pass the phone around: each player holds the card to read their role in private, then lets go. Put the phone on the table while the timer runs. At the end, reveal everyone together and decide who completed their mission. You can replay the intro anytime from "How to play" on the home screen.

The timer ended but I got no notification. Allow notifications for Busted in Settings inside the app, or in your phone settings. Without permission the game still works, you just do not get the "time is up" alert while the app is in the background.

How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings inside the app and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open Settings inside the app and tap "Manage subscription", or go to your App Store or Google Play subscriptions yourself. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I change the language? Yes, in Settings inside the app: English or Turkish.

The game does not start or says the pack is locked. Starting a round needs an internet connection. A premium pack needs an active subscription; tap "Restore purchases" if you already subscribed. Once a round has started it keeps working offline.

What data does the app collect? Very little, and never player names. See the Privacy Policy.
